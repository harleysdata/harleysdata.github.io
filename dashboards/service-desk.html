    Data

    UCI Incident management process enriched event log (Amaral, Fantinato & Peres), CC BY 4.0. A real ServiceNow export; group and category names are anonymised in the source.

    Method

    Each incident's final audit record decides SLA, because made_sla flips during a ticket's life. Time-in-state is the gap between audit entries. Python and SQLite pipeline with 9 automated data-quality tests.
