      Data

      BPI Challenge 2019 (van Dongen, 4TU.ResearchData, CC BY 4.0): a real SAP purchase-to-pay extract. Vendors, users and companies are anonymised in the source.

      Method

      A streaming parser turns the 729 MB XES log into tables. Each control is tested on the first time each step happened on the line. Lines are pre-aggregated by month, spend area, flow and company, so filtering here matches filtering the raw lines exactly.

      Code & Power BI model

      Parser, control logic, SQL vendor scoring, DAX measures and the write-up are on GitHub.
