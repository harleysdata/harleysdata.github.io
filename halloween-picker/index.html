Halloween Weekend Picker

A ~1,200-member Discord server wanted to go out together for Halloween, and the plans were scattered across chat threads. I built a free voting app so everyone could say when, where and what in one place, and the totals updated live for everyone.

How it worked

  1. One link, on any phonePeople opened the web app from Discord and picked the nights they were free, their areas, the kinds of events they wanted, and specific events from a curated list.
  2. A sheet as the databaseEvery vote saved as one row in a Google Sheet. A private voter key let people come back and change their vote without making an account.
  3. Totals that update themselvesA summary tab added everything up with formulas, so the group could watch the plan form instead of waiting for someone to count replies.

What the group decided

It ran in two rounds: first to pick the best night of the October 16–18 weekend, then to plan Halloween weekend itself.

What I learned

  • Make "maybe" an option. For Friday of Halloween weekend, 7 people answered maybe and 6 said going. A yes/no form would have hidden almost half the interest.
  • Let people change their minds. The voter key updated someone's existing row instead of adding a duplicate, without asking anyone to sign up.
  • Show the totals to everyone. A live summary meant nobody had to count replies, and everyone could see where the group was leaning.
  • Reuse what works. The same pattern, a sheet as the database plus a small Apps Script page, now runs the GTA Girl Lounge and my Belladonna check-in prototype.
