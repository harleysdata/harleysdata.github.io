I · Data analysis

Contingent Workforce Compliance Analytics

A bank uses thousands of contractors from dozens of staffing vendors. Who is non-compliant, and which vendors are the problem?

  • Cleaned 5,120 raw vendor records to 4,980: removed duplicates, standardized flags, filled missing rates.
  • Overall compliance is 62.1%; incomplete training is the biggest gap (787 contractors).
  • Four of the 10 worst vendors were rated Low risk, so the risk tiers needed to reflect real performance.

II · Statistics

Dealership Inventory & A/B Test Analysis

Why do some cars sit on the lot, and did the new website lead form really work?

  • Cars priced more than 6% above market took a median 67 days to sell, versus 51 for cars under market.
  • The shorter lead form lifted mobile submissions from 2.82% to 4.19% (p = 0.004); the desktop change wasn't significant.
  • Recommended shipping the new form while tracking the wide confidence interval before reporting a final lift.

III · AI evaluation

AI Answer Accuracy Evaluation

People ask AI tools about their rights at work. How do you measure whether the answers are actually right?

  • Built a test set of 15 Ontario Employment Standards Act questions, each with required facts and common wrong answers.
  • The scorer grades each answer Correct, Partial or Incorrect and explains why.
  • It caught all 8 errors planted in a deliberately flawed answer set, including US overtime rules and outdated parental leave.
