I · Service desk analytics

Why a third of ServiceNow tickets miss SLA

A real ServiceNow audit log: 141,712 events, 24,918 incidents. Where is SLA being lost, and what can change in the tool to fix it?

  • SLA falls from 78% to 14% as tickets are reassigned, and the drop holds within the same priority. Two-thirds of breaches involved a reassignment.
  • Built 11 assignment rules on the first 60% of the year and tested them on the rest: first-touch accuracy rose from 51% to 82% on the tickets they cover, with 138 fixed and 3 broken.
  • Critical tickets meet SLA 1.9% of the time, even with no handoffs. The SLA definitions need fixing before SLA is used as a team KPI.

II · Controls & vendor risk

SAP procure-to-pay control testing

A real SAP purchase-to-pay log: 1.6M events, 251,734 PO lines, €997M. Which controls fail, how much spend do they touch, and which vendors come first?

  • 8,887 PO lines (€108.5M) had a high-risk exception: the PO was raised after the invoice, payment went out without a goods receipt, or the same person ordered and received.
  • When an invoice is posted before the goods arrive, 97% hit a payment block. 88% of lines are set up to allow that, so the configuration itself is creating AP rework.
  • Scored 307 vendors with SQL percentile ranks: 17 High-risk vendors hold €82.9M of spend.

III · Systems integration

Fieldglass → Employee Central → ServiceNow

A contractor's details live in three systems. How do you keep them in step, and prove it's working?

  • Designed the flow with one owner per fact: Fieldglass owns the engagement, Employee Central the person, ServiceNow the access. Includes a 14-field mapping, sequence diagrams and a controls matrix.
  • Built a three-way reconciliation with 7 checks, each with a severity and an owning team.
  • On sample extracts with 591 planted defects it found 591 of 591, with no false alarms.

V · AI evaluation

AI Answer Accuracy Evaluation

People ask AI tools about their rights at work. How do you measure whether the answers are right?

  • A test set of 15 Ontario Employment Standards Act questions, each with required facts and common wrong answers.
  • The scorer grades each answer Correct, Partial or Incorrect and explains why.
  • It caught all 8 errors planted in a flawed answer set, including US overtime rules and outdated parental leave.
